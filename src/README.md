**Connected Clients** shows who is connected to your Unraid server now, and who was connected in the last three days.
- **Sessions**: web UI, SSH, SMB, NFS v4, WireGuard and Tailscale, with the client address and the connection path.
- **History**: a 24-hour or 72-hour timeline and the sign-in events, including failed sign-ins.
- **Read-only**: a small collector reads these sources every 5 seconds. It changes nothing on the server.
